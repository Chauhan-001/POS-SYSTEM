Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Restaurant POS platform ("we", "our", "the Service") collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you. It applies to restaurant owners, staff, and end customers who interact with the Service.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, phone number, email, restaurant name, User IDs, and hashed passwords or PINs.
  • Business data: menu items, prices, orders, bills, invoices, inventory, customer lists, and loyalty data you enter into the Service.
  • Device information: device identifiers, device name, operating system, app version, and login activity for registered POS devices.
  • Usage data: pages and features used, error logs, and performance data that help us operate and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service and your Account.
  • Process orders, bills, payments, and loyalty rewards on your behalf.
  • Generate reports and analytics you request.
  • Deliver transactional messages (receipts, payment confirmations) and, with consent, marketing communications.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access; maintain security.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

3. Legal Basis

We process personal information where we have your consent, where processing is necessary to perform our contract with you (providing the Service), to comply with legal obligations, or for our legitimate business interests, such as security, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Service providers who help operate the Service (for example, cloud hosting, payment processing, and messaging providers), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorized users of your Account, such as your employees, as configured by you.
  • Legal authorities where required by law, legal process, or to protect rights and safety.

5. Data Retention

We retain your business data for as long as your Account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards to allow for restoration or export, unless a shorter or longer retention period is required by law. You may delete data from within the Service at any time.

6. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including encryption in transit, hashed credentials, and access controls, to protect your information. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices & Rights

  • Access, correct, export, or delete your business data through the Service.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law.
  • Opt out of non-essential cookies and analytics where available.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for business use and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International Transfers

Information may be processed in data centers in your region or abroad. Where personal information is transferred across borders, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is protected in accordance with this policy and applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the Service or by email. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

If you have questions or requests regarding your privacy, contact our support team through the Service.
